# The problem:

We are a robot, tasked with retrieving a book by ISBN.

There is a cost to rotate (cost_per_rotation), a cost to travel (move_cost_per_distance), and a cost to scan a title (cost_per_scan)

Our goal is to retrieve the requested book with the minimal cost total of cost_per_roation, move_cost_per_distance, and cost_per scan.
Our solution, then, aims to minimize the total distance traveled, the total number of rotations, and the number of scans.
